# Anthropic Use Case Form - Personal Usage Guide

This guide helps you fill out the Anthropic use case form for personal projects, learning, or individual development.

## Form Fields and How to Fill Them

### 1. Company name
**For personal use, you can enter:**
- `Personal Project`
- `Self-Employed`
- `Independent Developer`
- `N/A`
- Your name (e.g., `John Doe`)

**Example:** `Independent Developer`

---

### 2. Company website URL
**Options:**
- Leave blank if you don't have one
- GitHub profile: `https://github.com/yourusername`
- LinkedIn: `https://linkedin.com/in/yourprofile`
- Personal portfolio website
- Personal blog or website

**Example:** `https://github.com/yourusername` (optional - can leave blank)

---

### 3. What industry do you operate in?
**Select from dropdown:**
- ✅ **Software Development** (recommended for developers)
- ✅ **Education** (if learning/studying)
- ✅ **Research** (if experimenting)
- ✅ **Other** (if none fit)

**Best choice:** `Software Development` or `Education`

---

### 4. Who are the intended users you are building for?
**Checkboxes:**
- ✅ **Check: "Internal users (employees, staff, team members)"**
  - For personal use, "internal" means yourself or a small private group
- ❌ **Uncheck: "External users (customers, clients, public users)"**
  - Only check this if you're building a public-facing application

**For personal projects:** Check only "Internal users"

---

### 5. Describe your use cases (Maximum 500 characters)

**⚠️ CRITICAL WARNING:** 
- **DO NOT mention the word "Claude"** in your description - it will be automatically denied!
- Use "Anthropic models" or "generative AI models" instead

**Recommended description for Advisor Sentiment Analysis tool:**

```
I am developing a sentiment analysis tool for analyzing financial advisor 
communications using Anthropic models on Amazon Bedrock. This is a personal 
learning project to explore AI capabilities for text analysis. The tool will 
process advisor-client communications to identify sentiment, emotional tone, 
and provide recommendations. All data will be non-sensitive and used for 
educational purposes only. No PII or proprietary information will be processed.
```

**Character count:** ~328 characters (well under 500 limit)

---

## Step-by-Step Checklist

- [ ] Company name: Entered (e.g., "Independent Developer")
- [ ] Company website: Entered or left blank
- [ ] Industry: Selected (e.g., "Software Development")
- [ ] Internal users: ✅ Checked
- [ ] External users: ❌ Unchecked (unless building public app)
- [ ] Use case description: Written (under 500 chars, no mention of "Claude")
- [ ] Review: Checked for spelling and completeness
- [ ] Submit: Clicked "Submit use case details"

---

## Important Tips

✅ **DO:**
- Mention "Anthropic models" or "generative AI models"
- Focus on educational/learning purposes
- Emphasize no PII or sensitive data
- Be honest and professional
- Keep descriptions under 500 characters

❌ **DON'T:**
- Mention the word "Claude" anywhere
- Include sensitive information
- Exaggerate your use case
- Submit incomplete forms

---

## After Submitting

1. **Wait for approval:** 15 minutes to 24 hours
2. **Email confirmation:** You'll receive an email when approved
3. **Test access:** Try using Claude models:
   ```bash
   python cli.py "test" --model anthropic.claude-3-sonnet-20240229-v1:0
   ```

---

## Troubleshooting

**If your submission is denied:**
- Check if you mentioned "Claude" - resubmit without it
- Make sure your description is clear and professional
- Ensure you selected appropriate industry and user type
- Try resubmitting after 24 hours

**If you haven't received approval after 24 hours:**
- Check your email spam folder
- Verify your AWS account email address
- Contact AWS Support if needed
